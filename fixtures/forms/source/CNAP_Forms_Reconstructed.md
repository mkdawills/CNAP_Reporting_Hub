## Appendix: platform additions that are not source wording

These come from the architecture review, not from Piestar. They are design, and their wording is
mine; edit freely.

**Core surveys — gate and non-use path**
- Gate: *Did you use the [Core] Core's services or facilities during this reporting period?* (Yes/No)
- If No: *Do you foresee a future need for the [Core] Core?* (Yes/No); *Would you like to be contacted
  about exploring Core services or building a relationship with the Core?* (Yes/No); *Optional: what
  service or research need might be relevant?*; *Optional comments*. Completing this counts the
  assignment as complete through the `core_non_use` pathway.

**NeuroSync — attendance gate and non-attendance path**
- Gate: *Have you attended NeuroSync during this reporting period?* (Yes/No)
- If No: *Why have you not attended?* (Schedule conflict / Topics not relevant to my work / Did not
  know about NeuroSync / On leave or away / Other); *Do you anticipate attending in a future semester?*
  (Yes/No); *Optional comments*. Pathway `non_attendance`.
