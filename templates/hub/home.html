Research cores

Three cores support CNAP research with specific services for members.

Coming soon

Core vouchers will help members use core services. Details will be posted here when the program opens.

NI

Neuroinformatics Core

Advanced computing, data science, and artificial intelligence (AI) support for CNAP. On Beocat, an EEG analysis that took 1,280 hours now takes about 20.

Services and charges
  • High-performance computing on Beocat, with 496 Core processors and 10 GPUs
  • Data analysis, statistics, and machine learning
  • Research software, workflows, and automation
  • Workshops in R, Python, and machine learning, plus one-on-one consultation

Schedule of chargesComing soon

Request analysisBook a consultationCONNECT Lab
CN

Cognitive Neuroscience Core

Human neuroscience, electroencephalography (EEG), and brain stimulation support for CNAP. The only COBRE-funded cognitive neuroscience and EEG core in Kansas.

Services and charges
  • Study design consultation and methodological guidance
  • Experiment programming, setup, and troubleshooting in the CONNECT Lab
  • EEG data collection with 64-channel EGI nets and mobile OpenBCI systems
  • Transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS and rTMS) combined with EEG

Schedule of chargesComing soon

Request instrument timeBook a consultation
BN

Behavioral Neuroscience Core

Behavioral research, animal models, and experimental neuroscience support for CNAP, in 51 rooms across more than 7,300 square feet.

Services and charges
  • Design and running of behavioral assays
  • Surgical and laboratory procedure support, including stereotaxic surgery
  • Full animal care, housing, and compliance documentation
  • Training, onboarding, and scheduling of space and equipment

Schedule of chargesComing soon

Request instrument timeBook a consultation or tour

CONNECT Lab

Cog + Neuro Network for Experimentation and Computing Technologies, a separate facility with support from two cores

Experiment assistanceThrough the Cognitive Neuroscience Core

Experiment programming and design consultation, hardware integration with EEG, TMS, eye tracking, and response devices, help adapting existing scripts, and shared experiment code and templates.

Data assistanceThrough the Neuroinformatics Core

Data pipelines and analysis consultation, workflows for large-scale and parallel analysis on Beocat, machine learning help, and workshops on analysis tools and methods.

Staffed drop-in room, BH 444, with four workstations for R, MATLAB, Python, JMP, PsychoPy, Unity, and more.Visit the CONNECT Lab
All core services
