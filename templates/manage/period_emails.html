{{ "The Hub sends three kinds of email for this reporting session, each on a separate schedule."|stack }}

  • The “your reporting session is open” email. It goes to everyone with forms on opening day, {{ sched.opens|date:"l, F j" }}.
    The opening email lists that person’s forms and the due date.
  • The reminder email. It goes every 4 days, from {{ sched.first_reminder|date:"F j" }} through the due date, {{ sched.due|date:"F j" }}.
    The reminder email lists only what that person still has left.
  • The past-due email. It goes every day from {{ sched.after_due|date:"F j" }}{% if sched.closes %} until the reporting session closes on {{ sched.closes|date:"F j" }}{% else %} until you close the reporting session{% endif %}.
    The overdue email says the forms were due and can still be turned in.
