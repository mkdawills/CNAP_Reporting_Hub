How to acknowledge CNAP

What the Stevens Amendment is. {{ "A federal law that applies to every NIH-funded project. Whenever a product of the work is shared publicly, it must say that NIH paid for it, how much of the project NIH paid for, and that the views are the authors’ own."|stack }}

Why it matters for CNAP. {{ "Each paper, talk, poster, and proposal that acknowledges CNAP’s grant, P20GM113109, counts toward the Center’s record with NIH. Products that leave it out cannot be credited to CNAP in the Research Performance Progress Report (RPPR) or at the site visit."|stack }}

NIH’s guidance on acknowledging funding (opens in a new tab) · NIH Grants Policy Statement, section 8.2.1 (opens in a new tab)
