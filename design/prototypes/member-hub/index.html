The Cognitive and Neurobiological Approaches to Plasticity (CNAP) Center studies how brains and minds change, and helps early-career investigators build independently funded research programs. It is a Center of Biomedical Research Excellence supported by the National Institute of General Medical Sciences of the National Institutes of Health, grant P20GM113109.
