# CNAP Member Hub homepage prototype (Round 4)

A static design prototype for review, kept apart from the application.
Nothing here is loaded by Django, and no template, view, model, or migration uses it.

- `index.html` is the homepage. Open it in a browser. The Scenario menu in the dark bar shows the page with nothing due, a quiet week, a busy week, long titles, and three photos with no funding matches.
- `system.html` is the design foundations: type, color, spacing, grid, dividers, buttons, navigation, status markers, and image treatments.
- People, talks, funding, posts, and dates are fictional samples.
- The research image is a generated illustration, and the photos are placeholders without people.
- Fonts are subsets of Inter and Lora, both under the SIL Open Font License.
